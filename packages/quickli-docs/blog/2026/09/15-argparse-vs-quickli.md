`argparse` is available everywhere Python runs and is a dependable standard-library choice.
quiCkLI offers a different teaching surface with explicit application, command, argument,
option, and handler abstractions.

{/* truncate */}

This is not a replacement story. `argparse` is often the right dependency-free production
choice; quiCkLI is an alternative for developers who want a smaller framework model and a
clear path through the design of a CLI.
