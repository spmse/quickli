quiCkLI is intentionally educational and fun. That does not make it irrelevant: small
projects can clarify ideas, provide a reference implementation, and grow into useful tools
when their boundaries remain honest.

{/* truncate */}

The project does not need to replace every mature framework to be worthwhile. It needs to
help someone understand CLI design, build a small tool, or decide what abstractions they
actually need.
