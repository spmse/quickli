Click is mature, widely used, and full of production-oriented capabilities. quiCkLI is
smaller and deliberately exposes more of the mechanics involved in command registration,
parsing, validation, and dispatch.

{/* truncate */}

The right choice depends on the job. Choose the mature ecosystem when delivery and breadth
matter most; choose quiCkLI when a small surface and learning value are part of the goal.
